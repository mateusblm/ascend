# Ascend Product Vision

## Positioning

Ascend is a habit-building app with RPG progression for people who want real-life discipline without the cold feel of a generic task tracker.

The app should feel like:
- a personal progression system
- a daily accountability loop
- a game layer on top of meaningful self-improvement

The app should not drift into:
- generic to-do list behavior
- project management software
- shallow gamification with no real progression depth

## Target User

Primary user:
- people between 16 and 35 who like games, anime, RPG progression, self-improvement, and visual progress systems

Secondary user:
- students
- gym and fitness users
- people building study or work discipline
- users who abandoned traditional habit trackers because they felt boring

## Core Value Proposition

Ascend turns daily habits into visible character growth.

The user should feel:
- stronger because progress is measurable
- motivated because the system rewards consistency
- attached because the character reflects their real effort

## Core Product Loop

1. User chooses a real-life objective.
2. App suggests or organizes quests that serve that objective.
3. User completes quests and earns XP, attribute growth, streak progress, and unlocks.
4. User sees visible evolution in stats, rank, title, and milestones.
5. User returns to preserve momentum and continue progression.

## Retention Drivers

The product should retain users through:
- daily streaks
- weekly boss challenges
- attribute and level progression
- unlockable titles, classes, and cosmetics
- visible historical progress
- medium-term goals such as 7, 30, and 90 day journeys

## Differentiators

The strongest market differentiators for Ascend are:
- RPG identity with meaningful progression, not just badges
- goal-oriented journeys instead of isolated habits
- emotional attachment to a persistent character
- strong visual feedback on growth
- AI-assisted quest planning and progression balancing

## Product Pillars

### 1. Discipline
- make consistency visible
- reward repeated effort
- reduce friction to completing a quest

### 2. Progression
- level, rank, titles, classes, talents, achievements
- make growth feel earned and cumulative

### 3. Guidance
- help users decide what to do next
- propose quests, plans, and weekly priorities

### 4. Identity
- avatar, class, build, specialization, progression history
- user should feel they are building a version of themselves

## Monetization Direction

Free tier:
- core quests
- leveling
- base analytics
- basic achievements

Paid tier:
- advanced analytics
- guided journeys
- AI quest planner and weekly coach
- premium classes, themes, cosmetics, and progression packs
- social challenge tools

## Product Rules

- Every new feature must improve retention, differentiation, or monetization.
- Do not add features that only increase surface area without strengthening the core loop.
- Prefer depth over breadth.
- Protect the feeling of real progress at all times.
