# Terms Of Use Draft

## Purpose

This document is the draft baseline for Ascend's public terms of use.

## Product Nature

Ascend is a progression and discipline product. It is not a medical, legal, or professional advice tool.

## Account Use

Users are responsible for:

- access to their authenticated account
- the accuracy of the information they provide
- using the product honestly

## Competitive Rules

Ascend may enforce competitive integrity through:

- backend-authoritative verification
- session/grant validation
- trust or suspicious-pattern review
- competitive reward or standing adjustments

These protections exist to keep competitive progression meaningful.

## Live-Service Changes

Ascend may change:

- reward tuning
- season behavior
- rank logic
- verification requirements
- operational policies

when needed for product safety, fairness, stability, or service continuity.

## Abuse

Abuse may include:

- exploiting progression bugs
- manipulating competitive validation
- using unauthorized automation
- intentionally attempting to corrupt ranking or reward systems

## Public Release Requirement

Before public beta or store launch:

- publish a public-facing version of these terms
- link them from the app account surface
- link them from the store listing and release material
